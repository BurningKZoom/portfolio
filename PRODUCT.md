# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Technical Recruiters, Senior Engineers, and Hiring Managers in the BI and Data Engineering space. They look for technical depth, architectural clarity, and strategic impact.

## Product Purpose
A high-end personal portfolio that showcases strategic BI impact and data infrastructure expertise. The goal is to establish authority and secure senior-level technical roles.

## Brand Personality
Bold, technical, and authoritative. A "Professional Brutalist" aesthetic that leans into deep blacks, high-contrast typography, and a raw but polished technical feel.

## Anti-references
White themes, pastel color palettes, overly "soft" or friendly consumer aesthetics, and generic, non-professional "AI-generated" templates.

## Design Principles
- **Monolith Authority**: Use bold, oversized typography and deep black backgrounds to create a sense of scale and permanence.
- **Structural Transparency**: Embrace "brutalist" layout elements—visible grids, raw borders, and monospace accents—to mirror the transparency of data infrastructure.
- **Strategic Impact First**: Prioritize the "impact" metrics (e.g., "85% faster") with high visual weight, ensuring the value is immediately apparent to technical decision-makers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast (WCAG AA/AAA) is a priority due to the black-dominant theme.
- Reduced motion options for the technical glitch/motion effects.
- Clean, readable typography scales for complex data-heavy descriptions.
